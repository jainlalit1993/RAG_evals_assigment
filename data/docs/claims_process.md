# Healthcare Insurance Claims Process

This document details how to file and process a healthcare insurance claim.

## Filing a Claim

1. Receive care from a participating provider.
2. Collect itemized bills and receipts.
3. Complete the claim form provided by the insurer.
4. Submit the claim form with supporting documentation.

## Claim Review

- The insurer verifies coverage and service eligibility.
- The claim may be accepted, denied, or returned for additional information.
- Denials must include a reason and appeals instructions.

## Common Claim Issues

- Missing documentation
- Untimely filing
- Services not covered by the policy
- Incorrect provider billing codes

## Appeals

If a claim is denied:
- Review the denial reason
- Submit an appeal within the deadline
- Provide additional supporting documents
- Request an external review if needed
