# Benefits and Coverage Guidelines

This document outlines benefit categories and coverage rules for healthcare insurance.

## Preventive Services

Covered preventive services may include:
- Annual physical exams
- Vaccinations
- Screening tests (e.g., blood pressure, cholesterol, mammograms)
- Wellness counseling

## Specialist Visits

A policy may require:
- A primary care referral for specialist visits
- Prior authorization for certain specialties
- Higher copayments for out-of-network specialists

## Prescription Drug Coverage

Prescription rules may include:
- A formulary list of covered drugs
- Tiered copayments or coinsurance
- Step therapy requirements
- Prior authorization for certain drugs

## Network Providers

- In-network providers usually cost less.
- Out-of-network visits may have higher cost sharing.
- Some policies do not cover out-of-network care except in emergencies.
