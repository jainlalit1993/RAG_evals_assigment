# Healthcare Insurance Policy Overview

This document explains the basic structure and key terms of a healthcare insurance policy.

## Policy Components

- **Premium**: The amount paid regularly to keep the policy active.
- **Deductible**: The amount the policyholder must pay out of pocket before the insurer pays.
- **Copayment**: A fixed fee paid each time a medical service is used.
- **Coinsurance**: The percentage of the cost the insured pays after meeting the deductible.
- **Out-of-pocket maximum**: The maximum total amount the insured pays in a year.

## Covered Services

Most healthcare policies cover:
- Emergency care
- Hospitalization
- Doctor visits
- Prescription medications
- Preventive care

## Exclusions

Common exclusions include:
- Cosmetic procedures
- Experimental treatments
- Non-prescription drugs
- Services not pre-authorized
